Hey, a huge thank-you for **almost 1,500 downloads**! I’ve been reading your feedback on Reddit, YouTube, and wherever you’ve shared your Thor setups. You asked for more freedom to set up your shortcuts, and I’m excited to share what came out of that.

- **Both analog sticks are fully customizable.** Hold Back to use any of the four directions on either stick, or assign actions to single clicks, double clicks, and tap-and-hold on L3 and R3.
- **Back is yours to set up.** On a fresh install, all four Back gestures start blank. Give each one any action you like, or leave it empty. Updating keeps your existing bindings.
- **More actions for your Thor.** Turn screens on or off, switch performance and fan modes, toggle vibration, and more. Put any action on whichever gesture or hotkey feels right.

Starting with **1.5.0**, you can install future updates right from the app. You can also save and load your settings under **Settings → Backup** and share your settings file with friends.
