## Frontend compatibility
- **ES-DE Companion** and **NeoStation**: their bottom screen steps aside while you use another app there, and comes back from its own card in bottom Recents.
- **Cocoon** keeps its bottom screen running out of sight, so its top screen stays in the dual-screen layout.
- Swap a game away and quit it, and the other app returns to its screen, so ES-DE and Companion end up where they started.
- **RetroArch** keeps the real controller after a swap. The controller focus now follows RetroArch automatically (experimental).
- **iiSU** isn't supported yet; Back Button Hero leaves it alone instead of fighting it.

## A new bottom Recents
- Looks and feels like the system's own Recents, with real app previews and the matching light or dark theme.
- Navigate both Recents with the D-pad, including **Screenshot** and **Close all**.
- **Close all** also clears apps locked in the launcher.
- Optionally list the apps from both screens.

## Smoother swaps
- Hold-to-swap moves the running apps instead of restarting them, so games keep their state.
- Picking an app in Recents opens it on the screen where Recents is open.

## Also new
- **Pause shortcut**: hold two buttons (L2 + R2 by default) to switch Back Button Hero off, for example when an emulator needs Back, and hold again to resume.
- **Per-app exceptions**: choose which gestures and shortcuts each app keeps for itself.
- Redesigned settings with grouped pages, plus defaults and unbind per page with a preview of every change.
