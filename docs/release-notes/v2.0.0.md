Thanks for all your feedback and Thor setups! 2.0 gives you more shortcuts and new ways to capture and control your games.

- **More shortcuts, with either hand.** Assign actions to A/B/X/Y, Home, Start and Select too. Use Home like Back, or give it a separate set of shortcuts in Dual mode.
- **Record your gameplay.** Capture either screen or both, with game sound where supported. Add microphone commentary and optional push-to-talk.
- **Replay button sequences.** Save macros, edit their button presses and pauses, then replay them with a button combination.
- **Aim by moving your Thor · Beta.** Use tilt or turning motion to control either stick, with adjustable sensitivity and calibration.
- **Find and configure shortcuts faster.** See your bindings on a drawing of the Thor, search for actions, and set their options as you choose them. A new welcome setup helps you get started.
- **Do more without leaving your game.** Jump to your last app, close background apps, control music, mute sound or put your Thor to sleep—all from a shortcut.

Last-app switching keeps each screen’s apps separate. Closing apps now shows how much memory was freed.

**Updating keeps your customized bindings.**
