Thanks for your feedback since 2.0! 2.1 makes shortcuts follow the screen you're using and adds a few long-requested controls.

- **Shortcuts that follow your focus.** Choose "Focused screen" as the target, and Home, app launch, your app list and favorites, Active App Control, screenshots, brightness, video recording and a new App Switcher option all act on the screen your controller is driving.
- **Controller focus: Auto.** Hand the controller back to AYN's Auto mode from a shortcut or the Ask prompt. In Auto, switching to your last app and Back act on the screen you last touched.
- **Confirm and cancel like Android.** A confirms and B cancels in every prompt, the bottom switcher and top Recents, in Standard and Xbox style. Each one now shows its button on screen.
- **Press a pad button from a shortcut.** Map any of the Thor's own buttons, from A/B/X/Y and the shoulders to Start, Select, Home and the D-pad, to a combo. It stays held for as long as you hold the shortcut.
- **Do Not Disturb hides pop-ups too.** With "Try to hide all pop-ups" (on by default), Do Not Disturb also hides toasts, notification banners and this app's own messages. Turning Do Not Disturb off brings them all back.

Cancel now closes top Recents reliably, even right after moving between cards.

**Updating keeps your customized bindings.**
