Two things you asked for. Thanks to u/zerocean and u/TophersTalk for the reports behind them.

## New
- **Switch active app** shortcut: assign it to any Back + button or D-pad direction to send just the app in front to the other screen, without swapping both. The app keeps running, and whatever was on the other screen stays where it is.
- **Gamepad focus follows the app** (on by default, per shortcut): the controller moves along with the app to its new screen. Switch it off to keep the controller where it was. AYN's Auto-lock stays Auto-lock either way.

## Fixed
- **Hold-to-swap respects your exceptions on both screens.** An app with Hold turned off in the exceptions now stays on its screen, for example your launcher below, while the other app moves over it. Before, it could still get dragged along.
- **You're told when a hold doesn't swap.** If an exception blocks the swap, a short notice says so instead of leaving you wondering. Normal Back presses are unaffected.
