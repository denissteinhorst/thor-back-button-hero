Available actions

Explore all available actions

Pick an action for your shortcut. Macros need a button combination.

Apps and navigation

  • Back and Home. Go back or return Home.
  • Focused screen. New in 2.1 Point Home, app launch, your app list and favorites, Active App Control, screenshots, brightness and video recording at the screen your controller is driving.
  • Launch an app. Launch a favorite on either screen or the focused one.
  • App switcher. Open top Recents or the bottom switcher, with apps from one or both screens, or whichever belongs to the focused screen. A confirms, B cancels, with the matching button shown on screen.
  • Previous or next app. Jump between recent apps.
  • Active App Control. Minimize or force-close on one or both screens, with an optional screen prompt. Restart is new in 2.0. Force-closing or restarting can lose unsaved progress.
  • Switch to last app. New in 2.0 Flip between the last two apps on the controller’s screen. Each screen keeps its own pair. In AYN’s Auto mode it follows the screen you touched last.
  • Close Apps. New in 2.0 Close all apps on the selected screens, or keep the apps in front and close only background apps. Confirmation is optional.

Screens and controller focus

  • Switch both apps. Swap what’s open on the top and bottom screens.
  • Switch active app. Move one app to the other screen. The controller can follow.
  • Controller focus. Pick a screen, toggle between screens or show a prompt. New in 2.1 Hand the controller back to AYN’s Auto mode.
  • Screenshot. Capture the top, bottom, or both screens in a single image.
  • Display power. Toggle either display, use both, or use just the top or bottom screen.

Thor controls

  • Volume and brightness. Adjust either or both screens. Toggle mute is new in 2.0.
  • Performance mode and fan control. Change profiles from a shortcut.
  • Controller style, direct power supply, vibration and forced landscape. Access these AYN controls directly.
  • Refresh rate and stick lighting. New in 2.0 Toggle 60/120 Hz or choose a rate. Turn either or both sticks’ LEDs on or off.
  • Virtual mouse. New in 2.0 Toggle AYN’s stick-controlled mouse cursor.
  • Status Overlay. New in 2.0 Show battery level, time remaining or until full, and temperature.

Android shortcuts

  • Quick Settings and notifications. Open either panel from a shortcut.
  • Power Menu and Standby. New in 2.0 Open Android’s power dialog or put the Thor into standby, optionally after confirmation.
  • Do Not Disturb and Mute Microphone. New in 2.0 Toggle priority mode or block the microphone for all apps.
  • Hide all pop-ups. New in 2.1 With “Try to hide all pop-ups” (on by default), Do Not Disturb also hides toasts, notification banners and this app’s own messages until you turn it off.
  • System settings. Jump to Wi-Fi and internet, Bluetooth, Night Light, Extra Dim, Display, Sound, Location, Cast, Do Not Disturb or Battery Saver.

Recording and media New in 2.0

  • Record video. Capture the top screen, bottom screen or both with game audio. Press again to stop; recordings stay in Videos/BBH-Records.
  • Optional microphone and push-to-talk. Add commentary, choose a hold button and lower game audio while speaking. Apps that block audio capture remain silent.
  • Media controls. Play/pause, skip forward or return to the previous track without leaving your game.

Macros and gyro New in 2.0

  • Macro library and editor. Record or add controller inputs, edit steps and pauses, reorder, undo and preview timing. Reuse named macros across hotkeys. Release the modifier to play; use the hotkey again to stop.
  • Gyro on/off and calibration · Beta. Steer either stick with the handheld’s tilt or turning speed. Adjust sensitivity, deadzone and axes, with a live preview and an optional hold button.
  • Press a controller button. New in 2.1 Press A/B/X/Y, a shoulder, L3/R3, Start, Select, Home or a D-pad direction for as long as you hold the shortcut.
  • Macros, button presses and gyro need AYN’s built-in root service; they do not require rooting your Thor.

Install – Easy Peasy

Get started in a few taps, straight from GitHub.

  1. Download the APK from GitHub on your AYN Thor (Android 13).
  2. Open it and tap Install.
  3. Enable the accessibility service. Follow the prompt to Android’s settings, then come back and pick your first binding.

Download the latest APK

The first-run setup lets you load useful defaults, start with only Back configured, or keep your existing customized settings.

In-app updates are available from version 1.5.0. Tap Check for new version to find and download an update right from Back Button Hero, then confirm the install in Android. On your first in-app update, Android asks you to allow installs from Back Button Hero.

Android shows a generic warning for every accessibility app; here's what this one does.

Verify your download

Every release is signed with the same key. Signing certificate SHA-256:

7f8ab05139a406a3d50ba6267c60228b90935f4c02f854c26d69cf1b275df3ee 

apksigner verify --print-certs thor-back-button-hero-v<version>.apk

The printed digest must match. If Android refuses an update, don't force it. Open an issue instead.

Frontend compatibility

Dual-screen frontends follow no common standard. Some draw their bottom screen as an overlay window, some as a normal app, and some take the screen back whenever anything else appears there. I tested each one below on my own Thor and aimed for the best result it allows, without touching the frontends themselves.

ES-DE + Companion

Works public testing

Companion stays one card away in bottom Recents while you use another app on the bottom screen. Overlay-based versions get the same treatment: the overlay steps aside and comes back exactly as it was.

NeoStation

Works public testing

The bottom screen is finally yours again. NeoStation's panel normally can't be covered by anything, but Back Button Hero moves it aside whenever you open another app there, and its own card in bottom Recents brings it back exactly as it was.

The one limit: the panel can't move to the other screen, so hold-to-swap can't take NeoStation along. Instead of half-swapping, Back Button Hero leaves both screens as they are and shows a short message explaining why.

Cocoon

Works public testing

Cocoon's bottom screen keeps running out of sight while you use another app there, so its top screen stays in the dual-screen layout. Its card in bottom Recents brings it back. If Cocoon reopens its bottom screen by itself, after standby or a game, it's moved away again.

New in 2.1.1 While Cocoon fills both screens, hold-to-swap uses Cocoon's own screen swap, so Cocoon trades its two screens itself.

RetroArch

Works public testing

Swapping, the switchers and the Back gestures work as with any other app.

A launcher per screen

Mostly fine

Android's own home screens stay where they are; a swap involving one is refused. For a launcher that runs as a regular app, add a Hold exception to keep it on its own display during a swap.

iiSU

Works public testing

New in 2.1.1 While iiSU fills both screens, hold-to-swap uses iiSU's own screen swap and follows the "Screen swap" button set in iiSU's settings. With iiSU's swap turned off, you get a short message instead.

Still open: iiSU reopens its bottom screen the moment any other app appears there, covering whatever you just opened. Back Button Hero notices this and leaves iiSU alone instead of fighting it in a loop. A proper solution is planned.

Something doesn't work?

Bugs are still possible, especially with other firmware or frontend versions. As a single developer, I rely on detailed reports to fix things quickly. A good report includes:

  • the frontend and its version
  • your Thor model and firmware
  • what you did, step by step
  • what you expected, and what happened instead
Report an issue

Frontend handling uses AYN's built-in root service, which every Thor ships with. Nothing extra is installed.

FAQ

Screenshots & gameplay recording

Can I take screenshots with a button shortcut?

Yes. Assign Screenshot to a shortcut and choose the top screen, bottom screen or both. Choosing both saves one image with the two screens together.

Can I record my gameplay on both screens?

Yes. Assign Record video to a shortcut and choose either screen or both, with game sound. Use the shortcut again to stop. Videos stay on your Thor in Videos/BBH-Records; recording also stops when the screens go off.

Can I add my voice to a gameplay video?

Yes. Turn on the microphone in the recording options. You can record your voice throughout, or choose a push-to-talk button so you’re heard only while holding it. You can also lower the game sound while you speak. The push-to-talk button is reserved for talking during recording and won’t reach your game.

Why is the game sound missing from my recording?

Some games and apps block audio capture. Their sound stays out of the recording even when you can hear it on your Thor. Adding microphone commentary is a separate option.

Shortcuts & playing

Can the same button do different things with Back and Home?

Yes. Turn on Dual mode to give Home its own shortcuts. For example, Back + R1 could take a screenshot, while Home + R1 starts a video. Home’s taps and holds can have their own actions too. These are examples; you choose the actions.

Can one shortcut play a whole button sequence?

Yes. Save the sequence as a macro, then assign it to a button combination. Record the presses or add them yourself, and adjust the pauses. Release Back or Home to start playback; use the same shortcut again to stop. Macros support up to 60 seconds and 200 steps and need AYN’s built-in root service.

Can I aim by moving my Thor?

Yes, with gyro control (Beta). Tilt or turn your Thor to control either stick, and adjust how strongly it responds. You can make it active only while holding a button. Calibrate the sensors before first use. This feature needs AYN’s built-in root service.

Can I move just one app to the other screen?

Yes. Use Switch active app to move the app on the controller’s screen to the other display. The controller follows by default; you can turn that option off. To exchange the apps on both screens, choose Switch both apps instead.

Can I turn off one screen and keep using the other?

Yes. Assign Toggle Display Power to a shortcut and choose the top or bottom screen. The other screen stays on. Use the shortcut again to turn the chosen screen back on.

Can I change volume, brightness or fan speed without leaving my game?

Yes. Assign those controls to shortcuts. Volume and brightness can target the top screen, bottom screen or both; keep holding the shortcut to adjust them faster. Fan control lets you choose a fixed mode or pick one each time.

Setup, compatibility & privacy

Will it work on my Thor (Base, Pro, Max512)?

It should. Nothing in the app depends on the Max variant. Only the Max (16GB/1TB, Android 13) has actually been tested, so a short report from other models is welcome.

Does it work on other devices?

Partly, at best. Screen swap and the bottom-screen switcher need a second display, and the shortcuts are designed for the Thor’s hardware buttons. Other devices are untested.

An accessibility service can see everything. Why trust this one?

Verify rather than trust: the source is public, releases are built and signed by GitHub Actions, and the app includes no advertising or analytics SDK. It acts on the configured Back/Home gestures and controller shortcuts. Other keys go through as usual. It doesn't read the text inside apps.

Does it need root?

No, your Thor stays unrooted. A few features use the root service that AYN's firmware already ships: Back + D-pad and stick shortcuts without pausing the game, brightness and bottom-screen volume, AYN hardware controls, controller focus, moving apps between screens, force-close and frontend compatibility. Where it isn't available, the app falls back to a simpler method or reports the action as unavailable.

What leaves my device?

Back Button Hero checks GitHub for releases and downloads the APK from GitHub if you choose to install an update. Your settings, app list and switcher previews stay on your device. Recordings and macros also stay on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Does it interfere with my games or emulator bindings?

Hotkeys run while their modifier is held: Back, or optionally Home. Unassigned combo inputs reach your game as usual. For an emulator that needs Back for its own menu, set a per-app exception or use the optional pause shortcut. During recording, a chosen push-to-talk button is reserved for the microphone and does not reach the game.

Does it work with ES-DE, NeoStation, Cocoon, RetroArch or iiSU?

ES-DE with Companion, NeoStation, Cocoon, RetroArch and iiSU work and are in public testing; with iiSU, other apps on its bottom screen are still covered by it. See Frontend compatibility for what works with each frontend and how to report a problem.

An app is missing from the switcher.

Android gives no way to read another app's task history, so the list is rebuilt from windows the service has seen since it started. Open the app on that screen once and it will appear.

Can I save my setup?

Yes. Go to Settings → Backup to save your bindings, timings, preferences, pause shortcut and app exceptions to a file. When you load it, the app previews every change before you apply it.

Will my settings survive an update?

Yes. Releases are signed with the same key, so updates install in place. A button you assigned, or deliberately left empty, keeps its state.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's a hobby project for a niche device, and Play distribution would add an account, a review process and policy obligations around an accessibility service. Side-loading from Releases is the distribution model.
