Do I need to root my Thor?

No. Your Thor stays unrooted. Some features use the root service already included in AYN’s firmware. If it isn’t available, the app uses a fallback where possible or tells you the action is unavailable.

Will it get in the way of my game?

Unassigned inputs still reach your game. Set per-app exceptions for controls a game needs, or pause your shortcuts with a button pair. A push-to-talk button is reserved for your voice while recording.

Can I take screenshots or record both screens?

Yes. Choose the top screen, bottom screen or both. Screenshots combine both screens into one image. Video can include game sound and optional microphone commentary; apps that block audio capture stay silent.

Do I need an account?

No account, ads or tracking. Settings and recordings stay on your Thor. Network access is used for GitHub update checks and downloads.
